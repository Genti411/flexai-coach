Medical Disclaimer

Not medical advice

FlexAI Coach provides general fitness, stretching, mobility, and wellness information for educational purposes only. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always seek professional advice

Always consult a physician, physical therapist, or qualified health provider about any pain, injury, or medical condition, and before beginning any exercise or stretching program. Never disregard or delay professional advice because of something in this app.

Stop and seek help

Stop immediately and seek medical care if you feel sharp, severe, or sudden pain; numbness, tingling, or weakness; swelling or inability to move a limb; dizziness, chest pain, or shortness of breath; pain after an accident or fall; or symptoms that worsen. If you think you have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.

Physical activity carries inherent risks. By performing any suggested activity you voluntarily assume all risk of injury and are responsible for exercising within your own limits.

AI-generated content

Some guidance may be generated by automated systems and can be incomplete or incorrect. Use your judgment and consult a professional when in doubt.

Privacy Policy

Privacy by design

The app is built to collect as little data as possible. You can use core features without an account. Anything you type about pain, soreness, injuries, or body areas is treated as sensitive.

Chat is not stored

Your messages and the suggestions you receive exist only in memory while the app is open. They are not saved to disk or any server and are gone when you close the app.

What is stored on your device

Only a consent record (that you accepted this policy and the disclaimer, plus the date) and optional profile preferences you choose to set (such as fitness level or goals). These stay on your device and are not sent to us. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.

Data that leaves your device

The app works fully offline using an on-device stretch library. Only when the on-device matcher cannot understand your request may the app send that single message's text to Google's Gemini API to generate a suggestion. No identifiers, profile data, or history are sent. If no AI key is configured, this never happens. We do not use ad networks and do not sell or share your information.

Your controls

In Settings you can Export your locally stored data as JSON, or Delete all locally stored data. Because chat is never stored, there is no chat history to export or delete.

Data retention

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. We keep no copies.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect their information. A parent or guardian should review this policy and provide consent where required.
