## Not medical advice

FlexAI Coach ("the app") provides general fitness, stretching, mobility, and
wellness information for educational purposes only. The app does **not** diagnose,
treat, cure, or prevent any disease, injury, or medical condition, and it is **not**
a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

The information and suggestions the app provides are general in nature and may not
be appropriate for your individual circumstances. The app does not establish a
doctor-patient, physical-therapist-patient, or any other professional relationship.

## Always seek professional advice

Always seek the advice of a physician, physical therapist, or other qualified
health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition,
pain, injury, or before beginning any exercise or stretching program. Never
disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you
read or received in this app.

## Stop and seek help

**Stop any activity immediately and seek medical care if you experience:**

- sharp, severe, or sudden pain
- numbness, tingling, or weakness
- swelling, or inability to move a limb
- dizziness, fainting, chest pain, or shortness of breath
- pain following an accident or a fall
- symptoms that worsen or do not improve

**If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number
immediately.** The app is not designed for and must not be relied upon in emergencies.

## Assumption of risk

Physical activity carries inherent risks. By using the app and performing any
stretch, mobility drill, or exercise it suggests, you voluntarily assume all risks
of injury. You are responsible for exercising within your own limits and for
ensuring an activity is safe and appropriate for you.
