If you believe content in the app infringes your copyright, send a notice to
gentian.hoxha91@gmail.com including:

1. your contact information;
2. identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed;
3. identification of the material in the app claimed to be infringing, with enough
   detail to locate it;
4. a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the
   owner, its agent, or the law;
5. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that
   you are the owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf;
6. your physical or electronic signature.

We will review valid notices promptly and remove or disable access to infringing
material where appropriate. We may also provide a counter-notice process consistent
with applicable law (e.g., the DMCA in the United States).
